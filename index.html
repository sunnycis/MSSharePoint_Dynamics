1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines the process for utilizing the Microsoft SharePoint & Dynamics 365 Pitch (Cheat) Sheet as a business development reference tool. The document serves as a quick-reference guide for BD team members to identify and qualify Microsoft business application opportunities with prospective clients.

2. Scope

This SOP applies to all Business Development team members, Sales Representatives, and Pre-Sales Consultants who engage with potential clients for Microsoft SharePoint, Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and related Microsoft business applications. The tool is designed for internal use during discovery calls, client meetings, and opportunity qualification.

5. Procedures

5.1 Pre-Meeting Preparation

  1. Familiarize with the tool: Review the card categories, badges, and structure before client meetings.
  2. Identify likely scenarios: Based on client research, anticipate which cards may apply.
  3. Prepare probing questions: Review the "Ask them" sections relevant to the prospect.

5.2 During Client Discovery Calls

  1. Listen for trigger statements: Match client statements to the "They might say" triggers.
  2. Select relevant card: Identify the corresponding card that addresses the client's statement.
  3. Deliver the pitch: Use the "Pitch line" as a starting point; adapt to the client's specific situation.
  4. Ask probing questions: Use the "Ask them" probe to uncover more details and qualify the opportunity.

5.3 Opportunity Qualification Guidelines
